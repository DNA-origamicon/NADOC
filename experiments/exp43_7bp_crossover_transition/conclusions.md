No conclusion yet. The experiment is prepared but intentionally not running.

Conclusions must distinguish:

1. topology-dependent one-step or finite-lag predictive skill;
2. relaxation-trajectory prediction;
3. equilibrium ensemble agreement; and
4. recovery of slow bend, twist, shear, and inter-helix correlation times.

Non-equilibrated frames are valuable for learning directed relaxation from strained
states, but they are not samples from the equilibrium transition distribution and
must not be pooled with equilibrium-candidate production frames without an explicit
phase label and weighting strategy.
