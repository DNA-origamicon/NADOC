# Completed-run assessment and N135 recovery — September 11

## Recovery completed; validation remains inconclusive

N135/1 fs round-2 replica 201 stopped because CUDA allocated space for only 105
particles per cell. Its 5.280 ns checkpoint and 5.285390 ns error configuration
have finite coordinates, maximum bonds 0.3577/0.3613 nm and independently computed
potential energies matching engine values to about 1e-13 internal units/bead.
This supports a neighbor-list capacity failure, rather than physical explosion.

`recover_n135.py` preserves the original allocation and starts a separate recovery
from the successful 5.280 ns checkpoint. Remaining duration is 74.720 ns at 1 fs;
checkpoint velocities are retained, but the stochastic thermostat RNG restarts,
so this is not a bitwise continuation. The failed 0.005390 ns tail is discarded.
The Hamiltonian, box, temperature, timestep and neighbor-list geometry are unchanged.
`max_density_multiplier = 135` makes the engine allocate 136 entries per cell:
all 135 particles fit even if they occupy a single cell. Startup confirms this
uses 0.18 MB for CUDA cells. No engine rebuild or force-field change.

Recovery PID at launch: 700293. Current records, source snapshot and checkpoint
audit: `workspace/peg_chudoba/scheduling_294_20260910/recovery_n135_20260911`.
The recovery completed in approximately 53.8 minutes and restored the historical
scheduler. Analysis uses the resumed 74.72 ns segment with the normal 10% discard
alongside the two completed 80 ns replicas, preserving three independent origins.
The original 5.28 ns is excluded from analysis; no new production allocation was
added. The 5,390-step failed tail/replay overhead is recorded separately.

The resulting N135/1 fs cohort has RMS Rg **2.9751 nm**, conservative SEM
**0.0758 nm** and minimum effective samples **16.8**. CPU equivalence remains
**inconclusive**, not a pass or demonstrated force-field disagreement.

## What the completed runs establish

All nine CPU isolated-chain lengths pass the existing 294 K, zero-tail,
literature-reconstruction engineering and sampling checks. This remains a
bounded claim about equilibrium chain dimensions, not the whole PEG model.

continuation after the initial 20 ns. Sampling/stability checks pass. The

0.06705 nm equivalence limit. The earlier 2 fs discrepancy was not sustained

not validate physical kinetics or prove absence of all timestep effects.

## Next decisions

The recovered three-origin N135/1 fs cohort remains poorly sampled. Decide whether
the remaining 2 fs extensions would be informative before spending that allocation. Apply the same capacity
provisioning to future N135 GPU inputs. For EOS, inspect pressure-dependent
acceptance and autocorrelation bottlenecks; test collective chain translations
or other ensemble-correct collective moves in isolated diagnostics if warranted.
Do not repeat blanket extensions of exhausted production allocations.

The advance is isolated-chain GPU consistency at N36 and a diagnosed runtime
capacity issue. Bulk osmotic/collective behavior remains unvalidated. Therefore
brush compression, PEG–gold/linker, PEG–DNA and electrode actuation are still
separate validation tasks. Final-journal numerical reference verification also
remains outstanding; current literature comparisons use the recorded reconstruction.
