4 Structural Comparison                                                                                    The radius of gyration Rg and the end-to-end distance Ree
                                                                                                         were used as representative chain properties in Figure S9.
We performed an atomistic REMD of the PEG 36-mer and
compared chain structural properties to the CG model.
The simulation box contained a single polymer chain and
5824 TIP3P water molecules; the simulation setup is de-
scribed in detail in the main text.
   First, bonded parameters, such as distributions of the bond
length, the bond angle and the dihedral angle were scruti-
nized. Virtual sites placed at the centers of mass of the in-
tended CG beads were used as reference points when evaluat-
ing the atomistic simulations. The CG model retains much of
bonded structural details as shown in Figure S8. Though the
dihedral angle distribution is more prominent in the atom-
istic simulations near room temperature, the proposed CG
model exhibits almost the identical distribution when com-
pared to the CG model of Lee et al. S1
