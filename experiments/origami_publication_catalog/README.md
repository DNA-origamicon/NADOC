## Scope and decisions

- First publication strictly before January 1, 2020. Use first online publication, not the year embedded in a DOI or the journal issue year. Indexed dates can have day/month defaults; resolve borderline dates against the publisher before accepting them.
- Include either a published staple list or a published caDNAno/scadnano path/slice image. A CAD image is no longer required when a staple list is available. Missing/unreviewed images have an unassigned score, not zero. Conceptual images retained from the original image subset are labeled as such; they do not establish a complete structure.
- Only standard honeycomb or square packing. Curvature produced through insertions/deletions in an otherwise standard packed lattice is allowed. Hinged components can qualify when their individual bundles use these lattices; polyhedral edge frames and trusses do not.
- Exclude DNA-brick assembly, trusses, wireframes, and non-standard/hybrid lattices. Explicitly brick-named variants are also omitted from mixed-design papers. Ordinary scaffolded rectangular bundles are not equated with scaffold-free DNA bricks merely because they have a block shape.
- Inclusion is **design-specific**, not paper-wide. The 2009 shapes archive includes an icosahedron, which is excluded. The mass-production, pScaf, and magnetic-actuation literature includes brick-named variants that must not be imported indiscriminately.
- Lattice assignments use published methods or visible lattice/crossover-grid evidence. `lattice_evidence` records the basis; assignments inferred from a diagram are not equivalent to validation of a recovered topology. Ambiguous designs stay in the screening backlog.
- Scadnano was introduced in 2020 ([original preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11841)); it supplies no verified pre-2020 records in this pass. Later scadnano recreations of older designs would be labeled reconstructions, never originals.

## Image-only reconstruction score (v2)

The score estimates how completely **the cited image alone** specifies the target origami's CAD topology. It is a provisional review score, not a measured reconstruction success rate. Exact nucleotide sequences are a separate recovery task. Staple lists, downloaded JSON, experimental microscopy, and the number of panels do not increase this score.

Each component is scored 0–4: 0 = missing, 1 = fragmentary/ambiguous, 2 = partly recoverable, 3 = mostly recoverable with specific gaps, 4 = apparently complete and legible. Coverage refers to the entire target, not just the visible fragment.

| Component | Meaning | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| Whole-design coverage | Fraction of the complete target represented | Multiplies the detail score by coverage / 4 |
| Scaffold routing | Full path, connectivity and termini | 25% |
| Staple routes and ends | Individual routes, crossovers, nicks and termini | 30% |
| Base register and indels | Base counts/offsets and insertions/deletions | 20% |
| Helix layout | Unambiguous helix numbering and lattice placement from the image | 15% |
| Legibility | Whether needed details survive source-PDF/image zoom | 10% |

Detail score = sum(weight × component / 4). Multiply it by whole-design coverage / 4, round, then apply both ceilings:

- Conceptual illustrations: maximum **15**. Partial maps or isolated components of a larger target: maximum **35**.
- The weakest essential dimension (scaffold, staples, register, or helix layout) imposes a ceiling: 0→15, 1→35, 2→60, 3→85, 4→100.

Thus a detailed crop cannot outrank a full reconstruction specification merely by showing sequences, a 3D render, and many views. A score of 100 still means “all required features appear visible,” not that a reconstruction has been completed or validated. Full-page previews are convenience images; vector source detail may be used, but other figures, text, or sequence files cannot fill gaps for this score.

Stable IDs now appear beside rank. Previous ranks/scores/components are retained in JSON/CSV. D028 contains antenna-component maps rather than the complete testing origami and is also capped. `scoring.py` is the scoring authority; `build_catalog.py` checks every score against it. Scores and detailed evidence are stored in `catalog.json`. Better published images can justify new records or revised reviews, with provenance retained.

## Staple lists, characterization, and imaging datasets

Every row now has three separate evidence columns, also exported as structured JSON fields and CSV cells:

- **Staple list:** availability for the named design, a source link/local file, exact table or page where verified, and variant-matching qualifications. Thirty records have a published list located; D004 has paper-level lists without a unique match to its conceptual crop, and D013 has no verified list for the specific tutorial object. “Available” does not certify every sequence or chemical modification against the image.
- **Structural characterization:** modalities and figure locations, with design-specific comparison notes. Microscopy of another design in the same paper is explicitly identified. Functional FRET/transport measurements and wide-field coating images are not mislabeled as 3D structural maps.
- **Imaging datasets / evidence:** verified public raw dataset links/accessions are separate from published figure collections and supplementary experimental movies. **No public raw imaging deposit was verified for these 18 publications in this pass.** Six papers explicitly offer additional data from authors on request. No requests were sent. Supplementary panels and movies remain useful references, but are not raw particle stacks, localization tables, or deposited density maps.

Two studies provide especially relevant cryo-EM evidence: the large size-selective nanopore (limited cryo micrographs plus negative-stain classes), and the nuclear-pore-mimic ring (cryo 2D averages, negative-stain tomography and published comparisons with protein MD). Neither is claimed to have a public raw-data accession or a deposited 3D reconstruction here. The plasmonic-mirage study provides 3D DNA-PAINT measurements, with localization bias explicitly noted for simulation comparisons.

Sequence supplements for the magnesium-free bundles (XLS) and membrane pores (DOCX) are downloaded and hashed. T-pore confocal transport videos and the large-nanopore liposome/drift-correction video are linked as functional experimental previews. Magnetic-actuation movies involve larger assemblies with excluded brick-derived levers and are not attached as datasets for the isolated in-scope components.

For quantitative simulation matching, verify the scaffold/staple variant, functionalization, buffer, substrate or membrane, acquisition geometry, and image-processing steps. Published stained projections and AFM topographs have different observables from cryo densities or localization coordinates. Per-row notes identify the relevant observable without suggesting that all modalities measure the same structure.

## Original image-subset search (historical)

The broader search is documented in [SEARCH_COVERAGE.md](SEARCH_COVERAGE.md). The following figures describe the original image-only pass.

[screening_ledger.csv](screening_ledger.csv) preserves 1,830 deduplicated records from 1,831 Europe PMC hits. Broad query:

```
(cadnano OR scadnano OR "DNA origami") AND FIRST_PDATE:[1900-01-01 TO 2019-12-31]
```

A focused `(cadnano OR scadnano)` query returned 147 records, 145 with PMC identifiers. Full-text XML was retrieved for 96; 49 requests failed. Text harvesting identifies figure captions, supplement links, and named repositories, but does **not** establish image eligibility. Search hits include reviews, citations, and unrelated designs. The 18 included publications were checked against actual source images, with supplementary web/author-site discovery for historical papers. [search_log.json](search_log.json) records this pass.

Coverage remains incomplete, especially non-open-access articles, supplements not indexed by text search, figures without recognizable CAD captions, and the remaining discovery backlog. Citation chasing, additional publisher indexes, and manual review of every potentially eligible supplement are still required before calling this “all pre-2020 publications.” `has_reviewed_images` in the ledger does not mean all figures in that paper have been reviewed.

## Original-file provenance

Author sources checked include:

- [caDNAno publication gallery](https://cadnano.org/gallery.html): 2009 NAR, Nature, and Science archives. Keep the source ZIPs intact; the extracted wireframe icosahedron is omitted from the file inventory.
- [ReuseDNA](https://github.com/stefanniekamp/ReuseDNA): paper-linked 6- and 24-helix templates. Git commit identifiers are pinned in the inventory. The publisher ZIP was also inspected; it contains a PDF rather than a set of JSON files.
- [pScaf](https://github.com/douglaslab/pScaf): paper-linked 6HB files downloaded; brick-named files omitted. The verified 6HB supplement panel is microscopy, so these files remain outside the image ranking until an eligible published CAD image is located.
- [Caltech's 2011 geometry-recognition supplements](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/386nr-f7943): Supplement 4 contains seven original `.cdn` design files. They decode as caDNAno `vstrands` JSON; preserve their original extension and bytes. Supplements 2–3 contain software/code.
- [Staplestatter](https://github.com/scholer/staplestatter): named in the large nanopore supplement; tree checked, no `.json`/`.sc` designs located. Software availability is not design availability.
- Plasmonic-mirage Supplementary Data 1–4: downloaded ZIPs contain **PNG maps**, not editable files.
- The living-animal robot's SI explicitly references an original JSON supplement, but a working download was not located in the retrieved publisher links. It remains `referenced_not_retrieved`.

“Not located” means not found in the sources checked; it never means the file is known not to exist. Later community recreations are not treated as author originals. Repository/download evidence is retained with the source assets. File validation here checks JSON decoding and a nonempty caDNAno `vstrands` list, not strand topology, lattice consistency, sequence identity, or experimental fidelity.

## Storage and reproducibility

Small tables, scripts, manifests, and this document live in `experiments/origami_publication_catalog/`. Bulk evidence lives under the ignored machine-local `assets/` directory or symlink; see [machine setup](RESEARCH_SETUP.md) for restoration and storage configuration. These local image/file links require that asset directory; a Git checkout alone does not include downloaded publisher material. Do not publish this corpus automatically. Original copyright/license notices remain in source documents; public redistribution rights are not inferred from download access.

`assets_manifest.json` records SHA-256 hashes, source URLs, and PDF-page derivations for every table-linked asset. Full-page previews were rasterized with Poppler at 1,800 pixels on the longest dimension; no routes were redrawn. Original PNGs and JSON/CDN bytes are retained unchanged. Raw sources, XML, download logs, and archive ZIPs are retained for future screening. Disposable contact sheets, unused page previews, and the malformed large text extraction were removed.

Regenerate tables from the canonical curated data:

```bash
python experiments/origami_publication_catalog/build_catalog.py
```

`curate.py` preserves the initial v1 recipe and refuses to overwrite a schema-v2 catalog. `enrich_catalog.py` records the v2 evidence migration. Edit the canonical JSON for routine future curation; rerun a migration only deliberately, because its curated fields will be reset to that migration’s evidence snapshot. `harvest.py` and `fetch_evidence.py` are research utilities for extending discovery and retrieving evidence; **they never auto-accept candidates**. Pass an archive-mounted directory explicitly, verify its mount first, and set `TMPDIR` there. They use Python's standard library. The initial manual acquisition also used the workstation's Requests and Pillow installations and Poppler, without changing project dependencies.

For each new candidate, FIRST apply the source gate in RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md:

For passing candidates: confirm first-publication date; review design/lattice eligibility; locate the actual published diagram; record figure and PDF page; inspect and score it; search repositories named in the paper and supplement; download and hash eligible original files; record unavailable sources and exact-vs-template matching status; regenerate tables. Preserve exclusion reasons so the same wireframe or brick paper is not repeatedly screened.
