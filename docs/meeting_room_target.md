# Guest QR invitations

Presentation → Passwordless link QR code displays the current design’s reusable guest invitation. Guests scan it and enter a name; ordinary link invitations can require a password. Admission limits and the waiting queue apply to both.

Presentation → Manage sharing includes **Print guest invitation**, a printable sheet containing the same guest QR. It does not request a camera after joining, encode a physical tracking size, or align devices in a shared coordinate system. The QR remains reusable after ending a presentation; resetting the link revokes old invitations.

QR-based spatial AR tracking, reference markers, Vive calibration, and cube exports were removed on 2026-10-16. Guest model viewing and live VR-avatar visualization remain supported.
